FAQs

Goodquestions

What does a GoHighLevel and AI automation agency actually do?

We connect your website, ads, phone and CRM so every lead is captured, answered and followed up automatically. Ainex builds inside GoHighLevel, then links it to Zapier, Make, n8n and your job software using webhooks and APIs.

How much does it cost?

The Lead Engine core package starts at $459 and includes a website of up to 15 pages, a GoHighLevel CRM build, an AI agent and the core automations. Meta and Google Ads management is a $199 add-on. Ad spend and platform subscriptions are paid directly to each platform.

Do I need to already use GoHighLevel?

No. We can set up a new account or work inside your existing one. If you run another CRM, we can often integrate with it instead. [CONFIRM: do you provide GHL sub-accounts?]

How long does a build take?

Small fixes can go live in an afternoon. Our CRM-to-ServiceM8 lead handoff for an HVAC business was requested, built and tested the same day. Full lead-engine builds typically take [X–Y weeks].

Can the AI voice agent book straight into my calendar?

Yes. It answers, qualifies, books, reschedules and cancels, and it follows up on missed calls. Call our live demo to hear it. For clinics, the agent handles booking and general questions only, never clinical advice.

Can you build an app for my team, not just a website?

Yes. North City Roofing's field team runs on an installable app we built: owner and inspector views, live analytics, a 6-step inspection form and two-way CRM sync. It installs to the home screen with no app store needed.

Do you work with businesses outside the Philippines?

Yes. Our team is based in Antipolo, Rizal, and we've built systems for clients in the US, Canada, Mexico, Ireland, the UK and Australia. We schedule calls in your business hours.

Do you offer white-label builds for agencies?

Yes. Our Done-With-You track covers white-label snapshots, workflows, A2P setup and AI agents built under your brand, with a shared Slack or WhatsApp channel and documented handover. Email nexsale.dev@gmail.com to talk partnerships.
