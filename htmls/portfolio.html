Valle Del Sol — Cabo's Secret Valley

A real estate & hospitality development above Cabo San Lucas — desert ranch homes, a working farm, and the award-winning Torote Restaurant. Built entirely from scratch in VS Code with custom HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and first hosted live on Vercel. Once the client needed ongoing, non-technical management, the whole build was migrated into GoHighLevel through vibe coding, preserving the original design and animation exactly.
