North City Roofing — From First Click to Signed Job

The Problem

Leads were lost before anyone reached a roof.

  • Website leads waited on a callback

    Someone still had to follow up, find a time, and book the estimate by hand.

  • Yelp leads stayed trapped in Yelp

    Many arrived after hours, often with no phone number. If no one replied fast, the homeowner hired the next roofer.

  • Every qualified lead was retyped

    Leads were copied into JobNimbus by hand, where sales runs its jobs. Slow, and easy to get wrong.

  • Five disconnected tools

    A CRM, paper forms, a separate calendar, an assignments spreadsheet, and a folder of PDFs no one could find.

  • Paper inspections

    Forms filled out on rooftops were retyped later. Nobody knew which jobs were worth pursuing for weeks.

  • 43,000 duplicate contacts

    Repeated CRM syncs made the contact list unusable.

System Map

One pipeline, from first click to signed job.

Layer 1 · Lead Engine

Every lead captured, answered, and routed in seconds.

17 live GoHighLevel workflows and 100+ custom fields handle each lead from the website or Yelp, day or night.

A booking-first estimate form

Eight services (roofing, repairs, gutters, cleaning, skylights, inspection, roof coating, and rejuvenation), plus a one-tap toggle for financing interest. On submit a booking calendar opens right away, so the homeowner leaves with an appointment instead of a "we'll call you."

A Zapier integration creates or updates each new Yelp lead as a GoHighLevel contact, with the Yelp Lead ID, the requested service, and the homeowner's full project answers. A bot engages each lead immediately, even after hours.

Deep Dive

The JobNimbus handoff

Qualified leads reach the sales team's job system with zero retyping and zero duplicates.

  1. TriggerA contact is tagged qualified, or its pipeline stage changes.
  2. Duplicate guardIf the contact already carries the sent_to_jobnimbus tag, the flow stops.
  3. FormatCustom code builds the payload.
  4. Route by sourceA branch checks the Yelp Lead ID, so Yelp and website leads each arrive with the right data.
  5. SendThe contact is tagged as sent, a webhook creates the lead in JobNimbus, and the team gets an SMS.
Layer 2 · Operations App

One role-based app replaces five tools.

The owner runs the business from a command center, and inspectors run assessments from the roof.

Owner command center

  • Team Performance: live KPIs for today, this week, and this month, plus 7/30/90-day analytics. A server-side Postgres function keeps it fast across 31,000+ contacts.
  • Top Performers: team members ranked by assigned and completed inspections.
  • Leads pipeline: every assessed contact marked Qualified or Not Qualified, with a full assessment drawer and CSV export. Status syncs back to the CRM.
  • Daily operations: Priorities, Contacts with tap-to-call, Team, live-synced Calendars, Appointments, Admin Booking, Sales Form, Announcements, and Files & Completions.

Member app

  • Assignments move through Start → Continue → Completed.
  • A 6-step wizard: project details, roofing material, wood damage, vents and flashings, materials and access, and final review. Large tap targets, autosave, and a sticky action bar make it usable on a phone in the sun.
  • Instant qualification: "Is this a qualified lead?" updates the CRM in real time.
  • Branded PDF reports are generated automatically, viewable in the app, and saved to the contact.

Technical Highlights

Built to stay fast and clean at scale.

  • CRM sync that scales

    Upserts run in 500-row batches on a unique contact ID, with deduplication before every write and a guard against overlapping runs. 43,000 duplicates gone for good.

  • Server-side analytics

    Chart counts run in Postgres, so the dashboard stays instant at any contact volume.

  • 100+ custom fields

    Mapped across GoHighLevel, the dashboard, and JobNimbus.

  • One webhook, one payload

    Each assessment sends the full scope, the PDF link, the qualification answer, and the inspector's identity in one request.

  • Real-time by default

    Supabase realtime keeps every view current without a refresh.

  • Safe handoffs

    Tag guards make sure no lead is ever sent to JobNimbus twice.

Results

What changed

  • No more lost leads. Website and Yelp leads land in one CRM with instant replies and booking, day or night.
  • Abandoned leads are recovered automatically instead of forgotten.
  • One app replaces five tools.
  • Paper to PDF in minutes. Inspections are captured on a phone, on the roof.
  • Instant qualification. The office sees the verdict the moment an inspector submits, not weeks later.
  • 43,000 duplicates eliminated and prevented from returning.
  • Zero double entry. Qualified leads flow into JobNimbus automatically.
