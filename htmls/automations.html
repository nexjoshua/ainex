Valle Del Sol Cabo · Featured Build

OpenTable → Make.com → GHL Reservation Campaigns

Every OpenTable reservation at Torote, the on-site restaurant for Valle Del Sol's development, flows into GoHighLevel and triggers a branching WhatsApp, email, and SMS sequence — one of several event-driven systems running behind the live site at valledelsolcabo.com →.

vds-automation-reservation-campaign.jpg
Reservation Reminder & Confirmation SequenceFires on a 7-day, 24-hour, and 2-minute countdown to every reservation — branches on guest reply ("Yes, Walk Me Through" / "No, Just Dinner" / timed out) and fans out into WhatsApp confirmations, backup emails, and an internal team notification the moment a guest responds. Delivery is tracked end to end on every channel.
vds-automation-opentable-make-scenario.jpg
OpenTable → GHL Contact Sync (Make.com)A scheduled Make.com scenario authenticates with the OpenTable API, pulls the live reservation list, iterates every guest, skips cancellations and no-shows, and upserts each one straight into GoHighLevel as a contact — the data source feeding the campaign above.
vds-automation-workflow-overview.jpg
Full Workflow, Trigger to OutcomeThe complete tree — tag-based trigger, opportunity creation, a venue/date condition split, then three parallel time-gated branches that each run their own WhatsApp → email/SMS → delivery-tracking sequence.

Live on valledelsolcabo.com — reservation reminders, confirmations, and internal alerts all run automatically, with no manual follow-up required.

North City Roofing

Internal Ops Dashboard Automations

Seven production workflows connect the roofing team's booking form, the internal dashboard, and Jobnimbus — all currently live.

nc-automation-formintake.jpg
Automation A — Form IntakeBranches new leads by urgency and source, tags and scores the contact, creates the matching GHL opportunity, and routes the notification path — all from one inbound webhook trigger. 6 live runs.
nc-automation-visitinspection.jpg
Visit Inspection CompletedFires when a field inspection wraps — creates the contact, updates the opportunity stage, and pushes the result to Jobnimbus via three chained custom webhooks. 28 live runs.
nc-dashboard-login.jpg
Contact to Dashboard (Supabase)Every new contact created by these workflows syncs straight into the Supabase-backed dashboard behind this sign-in screen — 35 live runs, the busiest workflow in the build.

Full run stats — Contact to Dashboard: 35 · Visit Inspection Completed: 28 · Calendar Form Webhook: 19 · Call Completed → Supabase: 16 · Form Intake: 6 · Inspection Assign Rep: 6 · AI Intake: 0. See the full case study on the Portfolio page →

Cross-Platform Builds

Two more from live client accounts — a smart-branching form webhook, and a Zapier bridge that keeps a GHL proposal in sync with a monday.com board.
